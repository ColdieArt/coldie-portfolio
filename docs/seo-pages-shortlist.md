# Pages shortlist — search & AI visibility

Draft, Oct 2026. 168 releases are in the database; **47 get their own page**, the rest stay on the
`/archive` timeline (each with a linkable anchor). Picked for: historic firsts and auction works,
famous subjects people actually search for, top sales, notable collectors, and enough substance to
say something original.

**Your part:** for each page, write the copy under *Write*. Aim for 150–300 words, in your voice. Lead
with one plain factual sentence (what it is, year, medium, where minted); that's the line search and
AI tools quote. Then the story: why you made it, how, and what happened (sales, shows, collectors,
press). Original text is what earns ranking. The SuperRare and OpenSea descriptions already exist on
those sites, so copying them adds nothing.

## Built for every page (no writing needed from you)

- **Basics:** title, meta description, canonical URL, share image (the thumbnail), and image alt text.
- **Structured data:** `VisualArtwork` with artist, date, medium, image, collection and provenance, plus `ItemList` on hubs.
- **Links:** breadcrumbs, links between series, subject and work pages, and a "View on SuperRare / OpenSea" link.
- **Discovery files:** sitemap entries and `llms.txt` entries, so AI crawlers get a clean index of key works.
